Featured build

Village Hall: an agentic service desk

A village hall is a small enterprise service desk: messy requests, specialist queues, and decisions only a human may make. I built an agentic front door that classifies requests, routes them to specialists with least privilege, and stops for a person before anything real happens.

▶ 2-minute walkthrough video goes here
Resident question→ Village Manager (orchestrator)→ Building & Zoning · Public Works · Clerk→ Inspector approves
  • Least privilege. Each specialist searches only its own chapters of the code. The orchestrator's only tool is delegate.
  • Grounded answers. Permit rules come from the server, not the model, and citations are checked against what the search actually returned.
  • Human in the loop. No model tool can approve anything. There is no approve tool to call.
  • Auditable. Every step, tool call and token count is visible in a trace under the answer.
